# CLCN Website Design Brainstorm

## Context
CLCN is a youthful, purposeful, welcoming, open-minded not-for-profit. Brand colours: primary red #D82F27, orange #FC5328, blue #009FE6, black, white. Fonts: Arial / Open Sans. Logo: two-line "Communication Language & Culture Network" in red.

<idea>
**Design Movement**: Contemporary Civic / Editorial Non-Profit
**Core Principles**:
1. Bold typographic hierarchy — large, confident headings in deep charcoal with red accent underlines
2. Asymmetric editorial grid — content blocks offset from a strict grid to feel dynamic, not corporate
3. Warm white canvas with strategic red and blue accents — never overwhelming, always purposeful
4. Human-centred photography framing — real people, real moments, never stock-photo generic

**Color Philosophy**: White (#FFFFFF) as the primary canvas conveys openness and clarity. CLCN red (#D82F27) is used sparingly for CTAs, headings, and key accents — it signals energy and action. CLCN blue (#009FE6) is used for secondary accents, links, and informational highlights. Near-black (#1A1A1A) for body text ensures legibility without harshness.

**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric editorial grid. Hero section uses a full-bleed left-aligned headline with a right-side image offset. Section dividers use diagonal red rule lines. Cards are staggered in a masonry-style offset grid rather than uniform rows.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Red diagonal rule / underline beneath key headings — references the brand's book/dialogue-bubble graphic
2. Large typographic pull-quotes in red italic — highlights CLCN's core philosophy statements
3. Subtle dot-grid texture on section backgrounds — adds depth without competing with content

**Interaction Philosophy**: Hover states reveal red underlines on nav items. Cards lift with a subtle shadow on hover. CTA buttons have a fill-sweep animation from left to right on hover.

**Animation**: Entrance animations use a staggered fade-up (translateY 20px → 0, opacity 0 → 1, 0.4s ease-out). Section transitions use a horizontal red rule that draws in from left. No excessive motion — purposeful and restrained.

**Typography System**: Headings in Arial Bold (uppercase for H1, title-case for H2/H3). Body in Open Sans Regular at 16px, 1.6 line-height. Pull-quotes in Arial Italic at 1.4rem. Nav in Arial Bold at 0.875rem uppercase with 0.1em letter-spacing.

## Selected Design: Response 1 — Contemporary Civic / Editorial Non-Profit

This approach best reflects CLCN's brand personality: youthful yet purposeful, welcoming yet professional. The asymmetric editorial grid avoids the generic "non-profit website" look while the restrained use of red and blue keeps the branding consistent and impactful. The human-centred photography framing aligns with CLCN's community-first mission.
